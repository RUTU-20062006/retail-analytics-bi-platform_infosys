1. Project Title & Domain
"Sir/Ma'am, our project is called OmniAnalytics(An Interactive Visual Business Intelligence Platform) under the domain of E-Commerce & Retail Analytics."

(If they ask what OmniAnalytics means: "It means an all-in-one, unified business intelligence platform that pulls together sales, inventory, and customer data into a single interactive dashboard.")

2. The Problem / Why We Chose This
"In traditional retail, businesses struggle with a few major gaps:"

Scattered Data: Their sales, inventory, and customer data are all trapped in separate, disconnected places.

Slow Insights: They usually rely on static spreadsheets, meaning they find out about drops in sales or stockouts way too late.

Complex Tools: High-end BI tools are too bloated and expensive for everyday operational use.

3. Our Solution
"We are building an interactive web platform that acts as a single source of truth. It takes raw retail data, processes it, and displays clean, real-time visual insights and trend forecasts so store owners can make quick, data-driven decisions."

4. Tools & Technologies We Are Using
Frontend: React.js, CSS, and Chart.js (or Recharts) to build smooth, interactive dashboards and graphs.

Backend: Node.js with Express.js (or Python FastAPI).

Data & Analytics Engine: Python, Pandas, and NumPy for processing data and forecasting trends.

Database: PostgreSQL to securely store transactional records and user data.
